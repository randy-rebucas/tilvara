# Privacy notice (draft)

> **DRAFT, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** For the DPO and counsel to adapt. It describes the system as built on
> 2026-10-18; anything marked [D1], [D2], [D6] depends on a decision not yet made. Publish only after the

Two parts: **A** is Tilvara's own notice for the people who use Tilvara (Tilvara is controller of account data). **B**
is a short template a business may adapt for its customers (the business is controller; Tilvara is its
processor). A Filipino version of each is needed before publication.

## Part A — Privacy notice for Tilvara users

**Who we are.** Tilvara is a point-of-sale and back-office service operated by [legal name], [address]
("Tilvara", "we"). Our Data Protection Officer is [name], reachable at [email] and [phone].

**Whom this covers.** Anyone with a Tilvara account: business owners, managers, cashiers,
accountants, and Tilvara support staff. Customers of a business are covered by that business's own notice
(see Part B).

**What we collect and why**

| What | Why | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your email address and the name you give | To create your account, sign you in, and let your business know who did what | To provide the service you or your employer signed up for |
| Your password (stored only as a one-way hash) and, if you set it up, your two-step sign-in key (stored encrypted) | To keep your account safe | To provide the service; our legitimate interest in security |
| Your sign-in sessions, including the type of browser or device you used | To keep you signed in, let you or us end a session, and spot a stolen session | Legitimate interest in security |
| A record of what you do in a business: sales you ring up, counts, approvals, changes | Your business needs to know who did what, and tax rules require a trail | Your business's legal obligations; legitimate interest |
| Security events: wrong passwords, lockouts, password resets, two-step sign-in changes | To protect your account and investigate misuse | Legitimate interest in security |

We do **not** keep your IP address in our records (it is used briefly to limit repeated attempts), we do
not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

**Who sees it**
- People in your business with permission to see the team or the audit log.
- Tilvara support, only when your business gives support access for a stated reason and time (at most 72
  hours, read-only). Your business can see every page support opened and end the access at any time.
- Service providers who run parts of Tilvara for us, under contract: hosting [D1: provider, country], email
  delivery [D2]. If our hosting is outside the Philippines, your data is stored there under the same
  protections.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

**How long we keep it.** Your account details while your account exists. Records of what you did in a
business are part of that business's records and are kept as long as tax law requires (at least 5
years), then made anonymous. Sign-in sessions and security events are kept for [periods from the
retention schedule]. Details: [link to the retention schedule summary].

**How we protect it.** Each business's records are kept apart by the database itself. Passwords are hashed;
two-step sign-in keys are encrypted with a key kept outside the database; owners, admins and accountants
must use two-step sign-in. Connections are encrypted. Backups are encrypted. Every change is logged.

**Your rights.** Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed, to access your data, to have it
corrected, to object, to have it erased or blocked, to receive a copy in a common format, and to claim
damages. Some records cannot be erased while the law requires a business to keep them; we will then
block them from further use and explain why. Contact our DPO at [email]. If you are not satisfied, you
may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

**Changes.** We will tell you of material changes in the app or by email before they apply. Version
[date].

## Part B — Template for businesses: notice to customers

*For the business to adapt and display at the counter or on its receipts or website. The business is the
controller; Tilvara is its processor. The business decides the final text with its own adviser.*

> **How [business name] uses your information at our counter**
>
> **Senior citizen and PWD discounts.** When you ask for the senior citizen or PWD discount, we record
> your name, your OSCA or PWD ID number, and your TIN if you give it. The law requires us to keep these
> details with each discounted sale for our tax records. We do not record your condition or any other
> detail from your ID. We keep these records for as long as tax law requires (at least 5 years).
>
> **Card and QR payments.** Your card or wallet details are handled by our payment provider; we never see
> or keep your card number.
>
> Our point-of-sale provider, Tilvara, stores these records for us and may not use them for anything else.
>
> Questions, or to see or correct your information: [business contact / DPO]. You may also contact the
> National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
