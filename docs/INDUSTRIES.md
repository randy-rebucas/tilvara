Retail Stores & Boutiques: Clothing shops, convenience stores, and specialty retail stores need a POS for barcode scanning, quick checkouts, multi-item pricing, and tracking stock levels across shelves.

Sari-Sari Stores & Groceries: Small neighborhood shops benefit from POS features like expiration date tracking, bulk-to-piece (tingi) inventory conversion, and low-stock alerts.

Beauty Salons, Spas & Barber Shops: Service-based businesses use POS software to manage staff appointment calendars, service packages, client history, and low-supply product alerts.

E-Commerce & Hybrid Retailers: Businesses operating both physical counters and online platforms use multi-channel POS systems to sync inventory and sales data in real time.

Pharmacies & Drugstores: Drugstores need batch and expiry tracking, senior citizen and PWD discounts with the required details on the receipt, prescription records for regulated medicines, generic and brand-name lookup, and selling by the box or by the piece.

Hardware & Construction Supply: Hardware stores sell by length, weight, and volume (wire by the meter, nails by the kilo, paint by the liter), need quotations that turn into sales, customer credit accounts and collections, delivery scheduling, and contractor pricing.

Auto Repair, Car Wash & Motorcycle Shops: Service shops use job orders that combine parts and labor, vehicle history by plate number, technician assignment and commissions, service packages (wash bundles, change-oil packages), and a queue of vehicles in service.

Water Refilling Stations: Refilling stations track container deposits and returns, route and door-to-door deliveries, regular customer accounts with prepaid or credit balances, and gallons sold per day.

Rental Businesses: Equipment, party and events supplies, gown and costume, camera, bike, and vehicle rentals need a POS for an availability calendar per unit with no double-booking, reservations with a down payment, hourly, daily or weekly rates, security deposits held and refunded at return, release and return with a condition check, late fees and damage charges, the renter's ID kept on record, and reports on what is out, what is overdue, and how often each unit is rented.

Bakeries & Pastry Shops: Bakeries need daily production batches made from recipes, waste and day-old markdowns, advance orders for cakes with a down payment and a pickup date, and wholesale pricing for resellers.

Clinics (Dental, Optical & Veterinary): Small clinics use a POS for appointments, patient or pet records, treatment and procedure billing with medicines and supplies used, installment payments, HMO or insurance charges billed to a third party, and follow-up reminders.

Gyms, Fitness & Dance Studios: Studios need memberships with start and end dates, session packages and walk-in passes, member check-in at the door, class schedules with limited slots, coach assignment and commissions, and reminders before a membership lapses.

Agrivet & Farm Supply: Agricultural stores sell feeds and fertilizer by the sack or by the kilo, animal medicines with expiry dates, seasonal credit for farmers paid after harvest, and wholesale pricing for cooperatives.

Gadget Repair & Electronics: Repair shops and electronics stores need job orders with the device's serial number or IMEI and its condition at drop-off, parts and labor on one bill, warranty tracking by serial number, and a status the customer can be told (diagnosing, waiting for parts, ready).

Printing, Photocopy & Internet Cafes: These shops charge by the page, by the size and color, or by the hour, need timers per computer or station, job orders for larger prints (tarpaulins, ID photos, binding), and quick counter sales of school and office supplies.

KTV Bars & Entertainment: KTV and billiard places charge rooms or tables by the hour, keep open tabs for food and drinks that run for the whole visit, need minimum consumable charges, and close the tab when the group leaves.

Canteens & Cafeterias: School and office canteens serve a fast line from a fixed daily menu, accept prepaid cards or IDs topped up in advance, may deduct from payroll for employees, and need limits set by parents or the employer.

Resorts & Day-Use Venues: Beach resorts and private pools charge entrance fees per head (with child and senior rates), rent cottages and tables for the day, sell food and drinks to guests, and take bookings for exclusive use with a down payment.
