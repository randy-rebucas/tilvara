Retail Stores & Boutiques: Clothing shops, convenience stores, and specialty retail stores need a POS for barcode scanning, quick checkouts, multi-item pricing, and tracking stock levels across shelves.

Restaurants, Cafes & Food Trucks: Food service businesses require POS systems designed for table management, kitchen ticket routing, order customizations, split bills, and fast counter checkout.

Sari-Sari Stores & Groceries: Small neighborhood shops benefit from POS features like expiration date tracking, bulk-to-piece (tingi) inventory conversion, and low-stock alerts.

Beauty Salons, Spas & Barber Shops: Service-based businesses use POS software to manage staff appointment calendars, service packages, client history, and low-supply product alerts.

Hospitality & Lodging: Hotels, resorts, and transient houses use POS and property management integrations to track room charges, guest services, and booking payments.

E-Commerce & Hybrid Retailers: Businesses operating both physical counters and online platforms use multi-channel POS systems to sync inventory and sales data in real time.
