Retail Stores & Boutiques: Clothing shops, convenience stores, and specialty retail stores need a POS for barcode scanning, quick checkouts, multi-item pricing, and tracking stock levels across shelves.

Restaurants, Cafes & Food Trucks: Food service businesses require POS systems designed for table management, kitchen ticket routing, order customizations, split bills, and fast counter checkout.

Sari-Sari Stores & Groceries: Small neighborhood shops benefit from POS features like expiration date tracking, bulk-to-piece (tingi) inventory conversion, and low-stock alerts.

Beauty Salons, Spas & Barber Shops: Service-based businesses use POS software to manage staff appointment calendars, service packages, client history, and low-supply product alerts.

Hospitality & Lodging: Hotels, resorts, and transient houses use POS and property management integrations to track room charges, guest services, and booking payments.

E-Commerce & Hybrid Retailers: Businesses operating both physical counters and online platforms use multi-channel POS systems to sync inventory and sales data in real time.

Laundromats & Laundry Shops: Self-service and drop-off laundries need a POS for job orders with claim tickets, per-kilo or per-load pricing, add-ons (detergent, fabric conditioner, ironing, folding), order status tracking from received to ready to claimed, down payments with the balance paid at pickup, "ready for pickup" notices to customers, washer and dryer usage, pickup and delivery, and a policy for unclaimed items.

Pharmacies & Drugstores: Drugstores need batch and expiry tracking, senior citizen and PWD discounts with the required details on the receipt, prescription records for regulated medicines, generic and brand-name lookup, and selling by the box or by the piece.

Hardware & Construction Supply: Hardware stores sell by length, weight, and volume (wire by the meter, nails by the kilo, paint by the liter), need quotations that turn into sales, customer credit accounts and collections, delivery scheduling, and contractor pricing.

Auto Repair, Car Wash & Motorcycle Shops: Service shops use job orders that combine parts and labor, vehicle history by plate number, technician assignment and commissions, service packages (wash bundles, change-oil packages), and a queue of vehicles in service.

Water Refilling Stations: Refilling stations track container deposits and returns, route and door-to-door deliveries, regular customer accounts with prepaid or credit balances, and gallons sold per day.

Bakeries & Pastry Shops: Bakeries need daily production batches made from recipes, waste and day-old markdowns, advance orders for cakes with a down payment and a pickup date, and wholesale pricing for resellers.
