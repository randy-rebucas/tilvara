# Tilvara — Feature audit (2026-10-19)

A full audit of the code against the product spec (`docs/SPEC.md`), done in session 19. It answers two questions:
1. Does what is built work end to end, from the database through the API to a screen?
2. What does the spec ask for that is missing or incomplete?

**Follow-up (2026-10-20):** sessions 20–22 closed every item in §3 that needed no outside decision, and session 21 settled §3.7 and the plan contents (see `STATUS.md`). What is still open is waiting on the user, the tax adviser, the accountant, the DPO, hardware or CI billing, and is tracked in `LEFTOVER.md`.

Defects found in built features were fixed in the same session and tested (§2). Everything still missing is listed in §3 and §4, with what blocks it. Open items also appear in `LEFTOVER.md`, which is the working list; this document is a snapshot.

### 2.2 Correctness fixes
- **Supplier invoice list status filter** (`payables.service.ts`): the list applied `LIMIT 1000` before filtering by status, so `status=PAID` could drop matching invoices past the first 1000. The status is now derived and filtered in SQL before the limit. Tested in `payables.test.ts`.
- **Payables aging time zone**: the default "as of" day was hard-coded to `Asia/Manila`. It now uses the entity's first branch's time zone, like every other local day.
- **Manual reversal of a supplier invoice entry** is refused with 422 `CANCEL_THE_DOCUMENT`. Before, a reversal by hand left the invoice open against books that no longer carried it, and a later cancel would fail on the second reversal. Tested in `payables.test.ts` and the E2E.
- **Till product search**: a failed search (network or permission) used to show "not found". It now shows the error.

### 2.3 Test-suite defects
- `reliability.test.ts` expected the `tenant.onboarded` payload without `businessType`. The session-17 business-type work added that field, but the test was never updated: a real regression in the committed tests. Fixed.
- `isolation.test.ts`: its setup builds two businesses with a row in every tenant table. It takes about 10 s alone and went past the 60 s hook limit late in a full gate run, so the file never ran. The hook now allows 180 s. It passes alone (10/10).

### 3.7 Defects found and left for a decision

| Defect | Detail | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Offline sales after a subscription lapse or suspension are quarantined** | `SalesService.recordOffline` runs through `mutate()` without `allowReadOnlyBusiness`. Sales rung up offline while the business was writable are refused (402 / `TENANT_SUSPENDED`) when they arrive after a lapse. While suspended they cannot even be retried or dismissed, and the admin has no wording for those codes. Nothing is lost, but legally required sales records stay unrecorded until renewal. That conflicts with §6.1: "billing failure must not corrupt an in-progress transaction". | Record deliveries of sales whose snapshot was issued while the business was writable (`@AllowReadOnlyBusiness` on the sync route, gated by snapshot time), and add the two codes to the admin's wording. It changes a security posture, so it needs the user's approval. |
| **Paying a restaurant bill skips the business-type and plan check** | `SalesService.createFromBill` does not call `assertFeature(db, 'restaurant')`; every other restaurant write does. A BILLED order can still be paid after a switch to RETAIL or a drop to Starter. LEFTOVER's business-type note says "switching back lets it be settled", which implies the opposite. | Keep paying allowed (refusing payment of food already served is worse) and correct the LEFTOVER note.
